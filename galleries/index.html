Questions

The ones we get asked

Where do the photos live?

In storage we run, served through a content delivery network so galleries load fast anywhere. Set a close date on a gallery and the photos and face data are purged after it.

What happens to a guest's selfie?

It is resized in memory, matched once, and discarded. Nothing about a guest's face is kept. Anyone can ask to have their photos taken down or left out of matching. The full text is at photos.slpmedia.pro/privacy.

Can I bring galleries I have already delivered?

Yes. Upload the exports as new galleries; the desktop tool takes a whole event folder at once and indexes faces as it goes.

Do my clients need an app or an account?

No. A link, a selfie, and their photos. Saving to the camera roll works from the browser on iPhone and Android.

Is my own domain required?

No. Every studio has an address on photos.slpmedia.pro from the start. Your own domain is a $19 a month add-on on Per event and Pro, and included on Unlimited: switch it on whenever you want it, add one DNS record, and nothing else changes.
