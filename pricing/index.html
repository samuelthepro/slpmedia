Questions

Before you pick

How does Per event billing work?

Creating your studio is free and there is no monthly fee. When you publish a gallery you are charged $49 for that event, which covers up to 1,500 photos. Larger events add $19 for each further 1,000 photos. Nothing is charged for a draft.

When does Per event stop making sense?

At three events a month. Pro is $149 and covers 15,000 photos across as many galleries as you like, so a studio delivering three or more events a month pays less on Pro, and a busy season never surprises you.

What counts as a photo?

Each photo uploaded to a gallery counts once, whatever size it is saved at and however many guests download it. Deleted photos are not refunded, but they do free up the count for the month.

What happens if I go over on Pro?

Uploads keep working. Each extra 1,000 photos in the month is $19, added to the next invoice. If that keeps happening, Unlimited is the cheaper plan.

How does the free trial work?

Pro and Unlimited start with ten days free. Set up your studio, deliver a real event, and if it is not for you cancel before the ten days are up and nothing is charged. After that the plan bills monthly or annually, whichever you chose.

Is my own domain required?

No. Every studio has an address on photos.slpmedia.pro from the start. Your own domain, such as photos.yourstudio.com, is a $19 a month add-on on Per event and Pro, and included on Unlimited. It is connected from your dashboard with one DNS record.

Can I change plans?

Any time. Moving up takes effect immediately and is prorated; moving down takes effect at the end of the billing period. Annual plans can move up at any point.
